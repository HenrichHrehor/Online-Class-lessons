Listening - Full Text (Jake's party)

A Friday-night problem story. Listen reads the full text aloud. Highlight grammar lines marks the sentences for section 3.

while + past continuous → Review A past simple → Review B present perfect → Review C

1. The Secret Party

It was Friday evening and Jake's parents were leaving for the weekend. Jake invited all his friends to his house, but he did not tell his parents. At seven o'clock Pete phoned him. "OK. They've gone," Jake said. "They went about ten minutes ago." Pete answered, "You can have the party at my house." Jake said no, because he wanted the music at home. While his friends were arriving, he put snacks on the table. He felt excited, but he was also nervous. He hoped the evening would stay simple.

2. While She Was Dancing

The party grew loud very quickly. Ruby was dancing near the shelf when her hand hit a small china dog. She stopped the music and looked at Jake. "I'm really sorry, Jake. I've broken your china dog. I knocked it with my hand while I was dancing." The dog was in two pieces on the floor. Jake picked them up and did not smile. That dog was not a cheap souvenir. It was a family object, and the accident happened in one second. Nobody spoke for a moment.

3. While They Were Driving

Ten minutes later Pete looked out of the window. "A red car has just stopped outside. Isn't that your parents' car?" Jake went pale. "Oh no! They've come back. Quick, everybody, hide in the kitchen." His mum and dad walked in with a small bag. "Hi, Mum, Dad. What have you come back for?" Jake asked. His dad explained, "Well, while we were driving down the motorway, your mum suddenly remembered her glasses. She turned round and came back." Jake nodded, but his hands were shaking. The music was still on in the living room.

4. The Family Dog

"Oh no, Ruby! That was my great-grandfather's," Jake said. "It's been in the family since the 1930s." Ruby looked down. "I'm really sorry." Mum smiled and said, "It's all right, dear. I'll get it for you. Where did you leave it?" Jake answered, "I left it in the kitchen." While Mum was looking for her glasses, the friends stayed very quiet. Jake is going to tell his parents the truth after the weekend. He has learned that one broken dog can end a party. The house was a mess, and the secret was no longer safe.

3. Grammar Review (lines from the listening)

Use the highlighted listening lines. Write the correct verb form.

A) while + past continuous

1) While his friends were (arrive) , he put snacks on the table.
2) I knocked it with my hand while I was (dance) .
3) While we were (drive) down the motorway, Mum remembered her glasses.
4) While Mum was (look) for her glasses, the friends stayed quiet.

B) Past Simple - finished actions

1) They (go) about ten minutes ago.
2) Her hand (hit) a small china dog.
3) She (turn) round and came back.
4) I (leave) it in the kitchen.

C) Present Perfect - the result now

1) I've (break) your china dog.
2) A red car has just (stop) outside.
3) They've (come) back.
4) It's (be) in the family since the 1930s.
