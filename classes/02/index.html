WORKSHEET - CLASS 02

Topic: About Me - Introduction (Curriculum Hour 01)

Listening - Full Text

Teacher / Natural Reader: use Show Full Text, then Copy Full Listening.

1. Hello, My Name Is Maya

Hello. My name is Maya and I am fourteen years old. I am from a small town near Bratislava, but I live in the city during the week. I am a student in year eight and I am usually friendly and curious. In English class I am not perfect, but I am ready to practise every day. My favourite colour is green and my lucky number is seven. When people ask about me, I smile and say that I am happy to meet new friends online and in school.

2. Family, Home and Pets

I have got a small family. I have got one brother and we share a room with posters on the wall. My parents have got busy jobs, but we have dinner together most evenings. We have got a quiet flat near the park and I have got my own desk for homework. I have also got a cat called Miso. He is soft and lazy. At weekends I am often at my grandparents' house because they have got a garden where I can read and play badminton.

3. School, Hobbies and Daily Life

I go to school by bus and my first lesson usually starts at eight. I like maths and art, but I love English videos about travel. After school I do my homework, then I play volleyball with friends. I do not watch TV every day. I prefer music and short walks. On Mondays I help my mum with shopping. On Fridays I meet my classmates online to practise speaking. These simple routines help me feel organised and calm during a busy week.

4. Goals, Personality and First Impressions

In the future I want to study design and I hope English helps me talk to people from other countries. I am sometimes shy at the beginning, but I ask questions when I do not understand. I am interested in photography and I take pictures of ordinary streets. If you meet me for the first time, please ask about my hobbies, my family, or my favourite place in town. I am glad to introduce myself, and I am ready to listen to your story too.

1. Grammar Review

A) am / is / are

1) I ___ fourteen.
2) She ___ from Slovakia.
3) We ___ students in year eight.
4) ___ you ready to start?

B) have got / has got

1) I ___ a brother.
2) Maya ___ a cat.
3) They ___ a quiet flat.
4) ___ you got a favourite hobby?

C) Present Simple (+ / - / ?)

1) Affirmative: I (live) in the city.
2) Negative: I (not watch) TV every day.
3) Question: (do / you / like) English?
4) 3rd person: She (go) to school by bus.
