Topic: My Story Spotlight — personal presentation (Curriculum Hour 24)

Listening - Full Text (model talk)

Leo gives a 2-minute spotlight presentation. Use Show Full Text for Natural Reader.

1. Opening: Who I Am Now

Good morning. My name is Leo and today I want to share my story spotlight. I am fifteen years old and I live in Kosice with my family. I am a year-nine student and I am interested in sport, music, and simple design. In this short talk I am going to introduce myself, explain one important past experience, and finish with my plans. I am a little nervous, but I am ready. Thank you for listening to my personal presentation.

2. Past Chapter: What Changed Me

Last year I joined a school project about local history. At first I felt shy and I did not want to speak. Then our teacher asked me to interview my grandfather. I prepared questions, I recorded his answers, and I wrote a short report in English. The presentation day was difficult, but I finished my part and my classmates clapped. That project taught me that practice beats fear. I also learned that real stories are stronger than perfect grammar.

3. Skills and Experiences

Since that project I have practised English more often. I have watched interviews, I have written short posts, and I have spoken with friends online. I have never travelled alone, but I have visited Bratislava with my class. These experiences help me sound more natural. When I make a mistake now, I correct it and continue. That is part of my spotlight story: progress is not a straight line, but I keep moving.

4. Closing: Plans and Message

Next year I am going to join the school media club. I am going to make short videos about teenage hobbies, and I will ask guests simple questions in English. My goal is clear: I want to speak for two minutes without stopping. If I practise every week, I will improve. That is my message. My story is still open, and I am excited about the next chapter. Thank you for your attention.

1. Grammar Review (for your pitch)

A) Present Simple - who you are

1) I (be) a year-nine student.
2) I (live) in ________.
3) I (not like) long boring talks.
4) What (do / you / enjoy) ?

B) Past Simple - one real story

1) Last year I (join) a project.
2) I (feel) nervous at first.
3) Then I (write) a short report.
4) My classmates (clap) .

C) Present Perfect + going to

1) I have (practise) English more often.
2) I have never (travel) alone.
3) Next year I am going to (join) a club.
4) I will (improve) if I practise.
