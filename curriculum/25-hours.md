# 25 Online Hours — Grammar Spine (Grades 8–9)

## Is it possible?

**Yes — as a functional school core. No — as “complete English grammar.”**

After ~25 online hours (≈19–25 teaching hours), a motivated grade 8–9 learner can **reliably use** Present Simple/Continuous, Past Simple/Continuous, Present Perfect vs Past Simple, *going to* / *will*, core modals, comparatives, and Conditional 1 — **if every hour recycles old grammar inside a new topic**.

They will **not** master every exception, advanced conditionals, full reported speech, or native-like accuracy. That is a multi-year school goal. This course builds a **spine**, not an encyclopedia.

**Design rule:** topic changes every hour; grammar accumulates. Listening + dialogue always force reuse of earlier forms.

## What “understood grammar” means here

Student can, without translation crutches for the forms above:

1. Ask/answer personal questions (About Me).
2. Describe habits and now-moments.
3. Tell a short past story.
4. Contrast experience (*I have been…*) vs finished time (*I went…*).
5. Talk about plans vs spontaneous decisions.
6. Give simple advice and rules.
7. Compare two things and make an *if*-plan sentence.

---

## Teaching pattern (every hour)

1. **Warm grammar** (8–10 min) — tiny review of recycle column  
2. **Listening gap-fill** (12–15 min) — topic carries new + old grammar  
3. **T/F + talk** (8–10 min)  
4. **Scrambled dialogue** (8–10 min) — spoken output  
5. **Grammar box + matching** (8–10 min) — metalanguage in SK/EN  
6. **Exit ticket** — 3 sentences using today’s form
